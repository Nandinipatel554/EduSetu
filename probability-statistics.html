Probability & Statistics

Learn the basics of probability and statistics step by step.

What is Probability?

Probability is used to measure the chance of an event happening. Its value lies between 0 and 1.

Probability = Favourable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

1. Sample Space

The sample space is the set of all possible outcomes of an experiment.

Example: When a coin is tossed:

S = {Heads, Tails}

2. Events

An event is a specific outcome or group of outcomes from the sample space.

Example: Getting a Head when a coin is tossed.

3. Basic Probability Formula

If all outcomes are equally likely, then:

P(E) = Favourable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

For example, the probability of getting a Head from a fair coin:

P(Head) = 1/2

4. Mean

Mean is the average of a set of numbers.

Mean = Sum of all values / Number of values

Example:

2, 4, 6

Mean = (2 + 4 + 6) / 3 = 4

5. Median

Median is the middle value when the data is arranged in ascending or descending order.

2, 4, 6, 8, 10

Median = 6

6. Mode

Mode is the value that occurs most frequently in a data set.

2, 3, 3, 4, 5

Mode = 3

7. Variance

Variance measures how far the values in a data set are spread from their mean.

Variance = Average of squared differences from the mean

8. Standard Deviation

Standard deviation tells us how much the values in a data set differ from the mean.

Standard Deviation = √Variance

Example

Find the mean of:

5, 10, 15

Sum = 5 + 10 + 15 = 30

Number of values = 3

Mean = 30 / 3 = 10
