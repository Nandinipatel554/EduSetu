Question 1

Solve the equation 4x - 5 = 19.

Given:

4x - 5 = 19

4x = 19 + 5

4x = 24

x = 6

Answer: x = 6

Question 2

Differentiate y = 2x³ + 5x² - 3x + 1.

Given:

y = 2x³ + 5x² - 3x + 1

Differentiating:

dy/dx = 6x² + 10x - 3

Answer: 6x² + 10x - 3

Question 4

Find the determinant of the matrix [[2, 3], [4, 5]].

For a 2 × 2 matrix:

Determinant = ad - bc

= (2 × 5) - (3 × 4)

= 10 - 12

= -2

Question 5

A fair coin is tossed twice. Find the probability of getting two heads.

Possible outcomes:

{HH, HT, TH, TT}

Favourable outcome = HH
Total outcomes = 4

Probability = 1 / 4

Answer: 1/4
