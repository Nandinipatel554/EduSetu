🔬 Physics Formula & Concept Sheets

Important concepts and formulas for quick Physics revision.

⚙️ Mechanics

Motion

Motion describes the change in position of an object with time.

  • u → Initial velocity
  • v → Final velocity
  • a → Acceleration
  • t → Time
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Newton's Second Law

Force acting on an object is related to its mass and acceleration.

F = ma

⚡ Work, Energy & Power

Work

Work is done when a force produces displacement.

Work = Force × Displacement

Kinetic Energy

Energy possessed by an object due to its motion.

K.E. = ½mv²

Potential Energy

Energy possessed by an object due to its position.

P.E. = mgh

Power

Power = Work / Time

🌡️ Thermodynamics

First Law of Thermodynamics

It represents conservation of energy in a thermodynamic system.

ΔQ = ΔU + W

🌊 Waves & Oscillations

Wave Equation

Wave velocity depends on frequency and wavelength.

v = fλ
  • v → Wave velocity
  • f → Frequency
  • λ → Wavelength

⚡ Electromagnetism

Ohm's Law

Current through a conductor is related to voltage and resistance.

V = IR
  • V → Voltage
  • I → Current
  • R → Resistance

🔍 Optics

Refraction

Refraction is the change in direction of light when it passes from one medium to another.

n = c / v

⚛️ Modern Physics

Mass-Energy Relation

Mass and energy are related according to Einstein's famous equation.

E = mc²

📏 Important SI Units

  • Length → metre (m)
  • Mass → kilogram (kg)
  • Time → second (s)
  • Current → ampere (A)
  • Temperature → kelvin (K)
  • Force → newton (N)
  • Energy → joule (J)
  • Power → watt (W)
