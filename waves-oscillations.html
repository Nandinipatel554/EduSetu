🌊 Waves & Oscillations

Understand waves, oscillations, SHM and important wave concepts.

1. What is a Wave?

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another without permanently transferring matter.

Examples: sound waves, water waves, light waves and seismic waves.

2. Oscillatory Motion

Oscillatory motion is a repeated to-and-fro motion of an object about its mean position.

Example: A simple pendulum moving back and forth.

3. Amplitude, Time Period & Frequency

Amplitude (A): Maximum displacement from the mean position.

Time Period (T): Time required to complete one oscillation.

Frequency (f): Number of oscillations completed per second.

f = 1 / T

SI unit of frequency is Hertz (Hz).

4. Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)

Simple Harmonic Motion is an oscillatory motion in which the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement and acts towards the mean position.

F = -kx

Here, k is the force constant and x is displacement.

5. Wavelength & Wave Speed

Wavelength (λ): Distance between two consecutive points having the same phase, such as two consecutive crests.

Wave speed depends on wavelength and frequency.

v = fλ

6. Sound Waves

Sound is a mechanical wave that requires a medium to travel.

Sound waves in air are generally longitudinal waves.

They consist of alternating regions of compression and rarefaction.

7. Example

A wave has a frequency of 5 Hz and wavelength of 2 m. Find its speed.

v = fλ

v = 5 × 2

v = 10 m/s ✅
