Question 1

Define velocity and write its SI unit.

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement with respect to time.

Formula:

v = displacement / time

SI unit of velocity is metre per second (m/s).

Question 2

State the law of conservation of energy.

The law of conservation of energy states that energy can neither be created nor destroyed.

It can only be transformed from one form to another.

Therefore, the total energy of an isolated system remains constant.

Question 3

Define specific heat capacity.

Specific heat capacity is the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of unit mass of a substance by 1°C or 1 K.

Formula:

Q = mcΔT

Its SI unit is J/(kg·K).

Question 4

What is simple harmonic motion (SHM)?

Simple harmonic motion is a periodic motion in which the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement from the mean position and is directed towards the mean position.

Its mathematical form is:

F = -kx

Question 5

State Ohm's Law and write its mathematical expression.

Ohm's Law states that, at constant temperature, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it.

Mathematical expression:

V = IR

where V = voltage, I = current and R = resistance.
