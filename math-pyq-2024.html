Question 1

Find the derivative of y = 3x² + 4x - 7.

Given:

y = 3x² + 4x - 7

Differentiating with respect to x:

dy/dx = 6x + 4

Answer: 6x + 4

Question 2

Evaluate ∫ (3x² + 2x) dx.

Integrating each term:

∫ 3x² dx = x³
∫ 2x dx = x²

Therefore:

x³ + x² + C

Question 3

Find the determinant of the matrix [[4, 2], [3, 5]].

For a 2 × 2 matrix:

Determinant = ad - bc

= (4 × 5) - (2 × 3)

= 20 - 6

= 14

Question 4

Find the mean of the numbers 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50.

Mean = Sum of observations / Number of observations

Sum = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150

Number of observations = 5

Mean = 150 / 5

Answer = 30

Question 5

Solve the equation 3x - 6 = 12.

Given:

3x - 6 = 12

3x = 12 + 6

3x = 18

x = 6

Answer: x = 6
