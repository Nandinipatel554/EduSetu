Mechanics

Learn the basic concepts of motion, force and Newton's laws.

1. What is Mechanics?

Mechanics is the branch of Physics that deals with the motion of objects and the forces acting on them.

It mainly includes motion, force, energy, momentum and interaction between objects.

2. Motion

An object is said to be in motion when its position changes with respect to a reference point with time.

Example: A moving car changes its position with time.

3. Distance and Displacement

Distance: Total path travelled by an object.

Displacement: Shortest distance between the initial and final position, along with direction.

4. Speed and Velocity

Speed tells us how fast an object is moving.

Velocity tells us how fast an object is moving in a particular direction.

Speed = Distance / Time

Velocity = Displacement / Time

5. Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time.

a = (v - u) / t

Where:
u = Initial velocity
v = Final velocity
t = Time

6. Newton's Laws of Motion

First Law: An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless an external force acts on it.

Second Law: Force is related to mass and acceleration.

F = ma

Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

7. Force and Momentum

Force can change the motion of an object.

F = ma

Momentum is the product of mass and velocity.

p = mv

8. Free Body Diagram

A Free Body Diagram (FBD) represents all the forces acting on an object.

Common forces shown in an FBD include weight, normal force, friction and applied force.

9. Example

A car starts from rest and reaches a velocity of 20 m/s in 5 seconds. Find its acceleration.

a = (v - u) / t

u = 0 m/s, v = 20 m/s, t = 5 s

a = (20 - 0) / 5

Acceleration = 4 m/s²

10. Practice Question
