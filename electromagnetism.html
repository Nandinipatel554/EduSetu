⚡ Electromagnetism

Learn electricity, current, resistance, magnetic fields and electromagnetic induction.

1. Electric Field

An electric field is the region around an electric charge where another charge experiences an electric force.

E = F / q

Here, E is electric field, F is electric force and q is charge.

2. Electric Potential

Electric potential at a point is the work done per unit positive charge in bringing it from infinity to that point.

V = W / q

The SI unit of electric potential is Volt (V).

3. Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

I = Q / t

The SI unit of current is Ampere (A).

4. Resistance & Ohm's Law

Resistance is the opposition offered by a conductor to the flow of electric current.

V = IR

Here, V is voltage, I is current and R is resistance.

The SI unit of resistance is Ohm (Ω).

5. Magnetic Field

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet or current-carrying conductor where magnetic force can be experienced.

6. Electromagnetic Induction

Electromagnetic induction is the production of an electromotive force (EMF) due to a change in magnetic flux.

ε = - dΦ / dt

This phenomenon was demonstrated by Michael Faraday.

7. Electromagnet

An electromagnet is a temporary magnet produced when electric current flows through a coil of wire.

8. Example

A circuit has a voltage of 12 V and resistance of 4 Ω. Find the current.

I = V / R

I = 12 / 4

I = 3 A ✅
