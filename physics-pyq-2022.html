Question 1

Define momentum and write its mathematical expression.

Momentum is the product of the mass and velocity of an object.

Mathematical expression:

p = mv

SI unit of momentum is kg·m/s.

Question 2

Define potential energy and give its expression near the Earth's surface.

Potential energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its position or configuration.

Near the Earth's surface:

PE = mgh

where m = mass, g = acceleration due to gravity and h = height.

SI unit is Joule (J).

Question 3

What is thermal expansion?

Thermal expansion is the increase in the dimensions of a substance when its temperature increases.

Solids, liquids and gases can undergo thermal expansion.

Question 4

Define amplitude of a wave.

Amplitude is the maximum displacement of a vibrating particle from its mean or equilibrium position.

It is generally represented by the symbol A.

Its SI unit depends on the physical quantity represented by the wave.

Question 5

Define electric current and write its SI unit.

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

Formula:

I = Q/t

SI unit of electric current is Ampere (A).
