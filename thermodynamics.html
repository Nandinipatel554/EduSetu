Thermodynamics

Understand heat, temperature, energy and the laws of thermodynamics.

1. What is Thermodynamics?

Thermodynamics is the branch of Physics that deals with heat, temperature, work and energy.

It helps us understand how energy is transferred from one form to another.

2. System, Surroundings and Boundary

A system is the part of the universe that we are studying.

Everything outside the system is called the surroundings. The separation between them is called the boundary.

3. Temperature and Heat

Temperature tells us how hot or cold an object is.

Heat is energy transferred from a hotter body to a colder body because of a temperature difference.

4. Internal Energy

Internal energy is the total microscopic energy present inside a system.

It includes the energy associated with the motion and interactions of particles.

5. Work Done

Work can be done when a force causes displacement.

In thermodynamics, work can occur when a gas expands or is compressed.

6. First Law of Thermodynamics

The first law is based on the principle of conservation of energy.

ΔQ = ΔU + W

Where:
ΔQ = Heat supplied to the system
ΔU = Change in internal energy
W = Work done by the system

7. Second Law of Thermodynamics

The second law describes the direction of natural processes and introduces the concept of entropy.

Heat naturally flows from a hotter body toward a colder body without external work.

8. Heat Engine

A heat engine converts some heat energy into useful work.

9. Example

A system receives 500 J of heat and does 200 J of work. Find the change in internal energy.

ΔQ = ΔU + W

500 = ΔU + 200

ΔU = 500 - 200

ΔU = 300 J

10. Practice Question

A system receives 800 J of heat and does 300 J of work. Find the change in internal energy.

Use:

ΔQ = ΔU + W
